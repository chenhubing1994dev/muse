# STYLE — 玄墨星火 / Mythic Ink Circuit

An original hybrid visual language created for Muse. It is not a copy of any existing film, game, logo or franchise.

## Essence
A mythic cosmos painted into being with Chinese ink gestures, then awakened by precise amber circuitry and retro-futurist signal geometry. The emotional contrast is between the fragility of one ember and the scale of a civilization.

## Palette
- Deep night: `#070B16`, `#101B2E`
- Rice-paper light: `#EDE5D4`, warm mist `#C9C1B3`
- Ink: `#11131A`, `#354052`
- Ember / knowledge path: amber `#FFB64A` and pale gold `#FFE3A0`
- Accent seal: restrained vermilion `#B8322B`, only at the climax
No rainbow gradients, no generic neon overload.

## Image grammar
- Negative space is essential. Keep one clear focal point per shot.
- Mountains, clouds, books and architecture are made from soft ink masses, dry brush lines and subtle paper grain.
- Light filaments and star-map nodes are thin, deliberate and sparse; they connect meaningful objects rather than decorate empty space.
- Pixel/CRT texture is a fine finishing layer: restrained scanlines, occasional signal ticks, subtle phosphor bloom. Never obscure faces or typography.
- The world is layered in foreground, middle ground and distant atmosphere; parallax gives depth.

## Motion
- The ember is the only continuous protagonist and visual through-line.
- Ink grows organically; it does not pop into flat vector shapes.
- Books float with weight, slow rotation and page flutter.
- Network lines illuminate node-to-node, never all at once.
- The signature move is a continuous camera flight along a golden knowledge filament, which exits the book-world and becomes a star bridge into the future city.
- Use camera push, lateral tracking, vertical crane, forward flight and final pull-back.
- Motion is smooth at 24 fps; selected ink gestures may resolve on twos.

## Typography
- Traditional Chinese title and subtitles, clear and high contrast.
- Subtitles sit in a quiet lower safe area, maximum two lines, never over the focal action.
- Use system CJK sans-serif fallbacks; no external font files are bundled unless their licence is recorded.

## Sound
- Cantonese female narration is the lead instrument: calm, mature, intimate, precise.
- Original plucked-string / guqin-like motifs, breathy flute-like tones, low drums and restrained electronic sub-bass.
- Foley is tied to the material: ember crackle, wet ink bloom, paper turn, filament chime, seal thump.
- Two near-silences create anticipation and release.
