# Campaign v2 — low-cost launch experiment

Status: **planning draft / internal review only**. This plan prepares a measurable first organic test; it does not authorize public posting, deployment, payment collection, or advertising.

## Commercial hypothesis

A short, practical AI-verification series can attract creators, freelancers and small teams who need a repeatable way to check AI-generated claims. The proposed funnel is:

1. Short video teaches one useful habit.
2. A genuinely accessible free one-page checklist gives viewers a next step.
3. The optional paid AI Verification Workflow Kit expands the checklist into reusable claim/source/counter-evidence worksheets and examples.
4. Feedback, support requests, refunds and actual net proceeds inform the next product revision.

This is a hypothesis, not evidence of demand or willingness to pay. The proposed US$9 price shown on the current product manifest is a test assumption, not a validated price or live offer.

## Seven-day organic test (relative days; schedule only after release approval)

| Day | Planned activity | Learning goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Publish episode 01 to one selected short-form channel | Establish baseline reach and first-3-second retention |
| 2 | Reply to relevant comments; record recurring questions | Identify audience language and objections |
| 3 | Publish episode 02 | Compare counter-evidence framing |
| 4 | Review retention and saves; do not change several variables at once | Identify where viewers drop or save |
| 5 | Publish episode 03 | Test uncertainty/credibility framing |
| 6 | Publish one text/carousel recap derived from the videos | Test whether a reference format earns saves |
| 7 | Review results and decide: iterate, hold, or stop | Use observed data, not intuition alone |

Do not assume a universal best posting hour. Start with the account's audience analytics once authorized; otherwise record the chosen time as an experimental setting, not a recommendation.

## Metrics to record per post

- Platform, account, post URL/ID, content version and publication timestamp (with timezone)
- Impressions/reach, views, average view duration, completion rate and retention curve when available
- Likes, comments, shares, saves and follows attributable to the post
- Profile visits, landing-page sessions, free-download starts/completions and consented leads
- Product-page views, checkout starts, paid orders, refunds, platform/payment fees and settled net revenue
- Production cost, paid promotion spend, local energy estimate if metered, and staff/review time

Missing metrics remain **unknown**, not zero. Do not combine views across platforms without noting different definitions.

## Decision rules

- Reach without meaningful retention: revise opening hook or pacing before increasing posting volume.
- Retention without saves/shares/profile visits: improve practical utility and CTA.
- Clicks without downloads: inspect landing-page clarity, download reliability and privacy friction.
- Downloads without paid conversion: interview consenting users, inspect product usefulness and price assumptions; do not immediately buy ads.
- Paid orders with high refunds/support burden: pause promotion and fix product quality before scaling.
- Scale only after repeatable organic evidence and positive net contribution after fees, refunds and production costs.

## Zero-budget operating policy

- Start with organic distribution only; no paid ads, paid stock, paid API, influencer fees or subscriptions are included in this plan.
- Use existing locally generated video masters and existing product assets only after rights review.
- Do not upload customer data, private research or local project files to social platforms.
- Do not collect email addresses or other personal information until the form, purpose, consent language, retention and deletion process are reviewed.
- No account connection, upload, scheduling, public deployment or payment configuration is performed by this document.

## Success definition

A successful first experiment is not “a video was generated” or “views increased”. It is a repeatable, rights-cleared content-to-download-to-product path with measurable audience value, transparent costs, low refund/support burden and positive settled net revenue. Until those events occur, revenue is reported as **US$0 recorded**.
