**Title:** AI 讲得很肯定，就一定是真的吗？记住这三步

**Description:**
AI 回答流畅、有自信，不代表每个细节都有证据。试试这三个习惯：
1. 把人名、日期、数字和引文拆开核对；
2. 回到原始来源，检查上下文；
3. 主动寻找反面证据。

找不到可靠来源，就明确标记「未证实」。这是一套实用查证方法，不是自动事实裁判。

## LinkedIn

**Post:**
A confident AI answer is not the same as a verified claim.

A lightweight review habit for research, proposals and client-facing content:
1. Break the answer into atomic claims.
2. Trace each important claim to an original source.
3. Search for counter-evidence and alternative explanations.

If reliable evidence is missing, label the claim unverified. The goal is not to make AI sound more certain; it is to make our work more auditable.
